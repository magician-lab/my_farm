Features

Everything Your Farm Needs

From the milking shed to the balance sheet — a complete ERP for dairy operations.

Milk Production

Daily morning, noon and evening milk recording with automatic totals, averages and production trends per farm.

Livestock Management

Animal registry, sheds, insemination and calving tracking, treatment history and health status monitoring.

Feed & Orders

Order feeds per farm, track deliveries, payments and balances with full order-to-payment visibility.

Finance & Reporting

Revenue, expenses, transactions and car sales analytics. Export professional PDF reports anytime.

Multi-Farm Support

Add unlimited farms. Each farm works independently with its own records, users and dashboards.

Role-Based Access

Farm users see only their farm. Admins manage everything — users, farms and all records.
